Privacy and advertising choices

Updated 30 October 2026. Operated by Kymata Labs LLC.

This notice covers this public index. It does not replace the separate notices for other Kymata products.

Reading and filtering this index

Website hosting and protection services receive technical request data such as IP address, requested URL and browser information needed to deliver and protect the site. Filtering and sorting the public directory do not send your search terms to an advertising service. Links to other sites take you to services with their own privacy practices. The index may load font resources from Google Fonts; those requests disclose technical request information to that service.

Advertising status and choices

Ad requests are currently held while site approval and consent configuration are incomplete. A seller authorization file does not mean ads are serving. If advertising is released, labeled Google AdSense placements may process cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses and page context for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent and opt-out choices are handled through Google's published privacy message where applicable. Advertising is kept separate from the index's ranking and inclusion logic; sponsorship does not buy a score.

The advertising privacy choices control is shown on the index when Google's message API is available. It reopens the consent message. Where applicable, Google's separate Do Not Sell or Share link handles US opt-out choices. Browser controls can clear stored choices; clearing a record does not delete data already received by a provider. No optional analytics tag is added by this advertising integration.
